Chronicles of Liberty

A journey through pivotal moments that forged a sovereign republic.

1857

The First War of Independence

The Sepoy Mutiny sparked a national awakening, uniting diverse regions against colonial authority.

1920

Non-Cooperation Movement

Mahatma Gandhi mobilized millions through non-violent resistance, boycott of foreign goods, and civil solidarity.

1930

Dandi Salt March

A 240-mile march asserting the fundamental right to natural resources, shaking the economic foundation of imperial rule.

1942

Quit India Movement

The definitive call to "Do or Die," demanding immediate and complete British withdrawal from Indian soil.

1947

The Midnight Tryst

At the stroke of midnight, India awoke to freedom and democracy, inaugurating a new era for Asia and the world.
